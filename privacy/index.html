Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guerra Pixel Studio handles information in connection with its website and games, including Mawkey The Last Macaw.

1. Information We Collect

Guerra Pixel Studio does not require players to create a separate Guerra Pixel Studio account and does not operate its own player account database.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not intentionally collect personal information directly through this website unless you voluntarily contact us by email.

If you contact Guerra Pixel Studio by email, we may receive:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if you choose to provide it.
  • The contents of your message.
  • Any information or files you voluntarily include.

The website hosting provider may also process limited technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, connection information, and server logs when necessary to operate and secure the website.

2. Epic Games Store and Epic Online Services

The Epic Games Store version of Mawkey The Last Macaw uses Epic Account Services and Epic Online Services (EOS) to provide platform functionality such as player authentication and achievements.

When these services are used, Mawkey The Last Macaw may access or process information provided by Epic Games, including:

  • Epic Account ID or other Epic platform identifiers.
  • Epic Games display name.
  • Language information.
  • Linked account display names when permission has been granted.
  • Authentication information required to identify the current player.
  • Achievement progress and achievement unlock status.

This information is obtained through Epic Games services when the player launches or uses Epic-enabled features in Mawkey The Last Macaw.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not receive or have access to your Epic Games password or payment card information.

3. How We Use Information

Information obtained through Epic Games services is used only when necessary to provide functionality related to Mawkey The Last Macaw.

This may include:

  • Authenticating the player with Epic Games.
  • Identifying the currently signed-in Epic Games user.
  • Displaying or using the player's Epic Games account information where permitted.
  • Providing Epic Games achievements.
  • Recording achievement progress and achievement unlocks.
  • Providing other Epic Games platform functionality.
  • Diagnosing technical problems.
  • Responding to player support requests.
  • Protecting the game and its services from misuse where applicable.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not use Epic Games account information for behavioural advertising, profiling, or unrelated marketing.

4. How Information Is Shared

Guerra Pixel Studio does not sell or rent personal information.

Information required for Epic Games functionality may be transmitted to or processed by Epic Games through Epic Account Services, Epic Online Services, and the Epic Games Store.

Epic Games processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

You can review the Epic Games Privacy Policy here:

Epic Games Privacy Policy

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or a valid request from a competent authority.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not share player information with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Guerra Pixel Studio does not currently maintain its own server-side database containing Epic Games player profiles.

Epic account identifiers and related information may be processed temporarily while Mawkey The Last Macaw is running when necessary to authenticate the player and provide Epic Games functionality.

Achievement data, Epic account information, and other information stored through Epic Games services may be retained by Epic Games according to Epic Games' own privacy and data-retention practices.

Emails sent directly to Guerra Pixel Studio may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, provide support, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Information controlled directly by Guerra Pixel Studio that is no longer reasonably necessary will be deleted when practicable.

6. Accessing, Correcting or Deleting Your Information

You may contact Guerra Pixel Studio if you would like to:

  • Ask what personal information Guerra Pixel Studio holds about you.
  • Request correction of information controlled by Guerra Pixel Studio.
  • Request deletion of information controlled by Guerra Pixel Studio.
  • Ask how your information is used.
  • Raise a privacy-related concern.

Requests can be sent to: eduardo@guerrapixelstudio.com

Guerra Pixel Studio may request enough information to reasonably verify your request before taking action.

Information held directly by Epic Games, including Epic Games account information, may need to be accessed, corrected, or deleted through Epic Games' own account and privacy services.

7. Other Platforms

Mawkey The Last Macaw may also be distributed through other platforms such as Steam.

These platforms may separately process information including account identifiers, purchase information, achievement information, device information, or other platform-related data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not control the independent privacy practices of these platforms.

8. Website Hosting

The Guerra Pixel Studio website is hosted using third-party infrastructure.

Hosting providers may process limited technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, connection information, and server logs when necessary to deliver, maintain, and secure the website.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Guerra Pixel Studio does not currently use its own advertising cookies or behavioural tracking technologies on this website.

Third-party infrastructure used to host or operate the website may process limited technical information necessary to provide its services.

10. Children's Privacy

Guerra Pixel Studio does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website.

Where Epic Games or another platform provides account or platform services, that platform's respective privacy, parental control, and age-related policies apply to information processed through those services.

11. Security

Guerra Pixel Studio takes reasonable measures to limit access to personal information and to use information only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

However, no internet-based service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our games, website, platform integrations, services, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be available at:
