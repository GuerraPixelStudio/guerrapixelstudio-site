Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Guerra Pixel Studio does not require players to create a separate studio account and does not currently operate its own player-account database. We do not intentionally collect personal information through this website unless you choose to contact us.

If you email us, we may receive your email address, your name if you include it, the contents of your message and any information or files you choose to send. Website hosting providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, connection information and server logs to deliver and secure the site.

2. Epic Games Services

The Epic Games Store version of Mawkey The Last Macaw may use Epic Account Services and Epic Online Services for platform features such as player authentication and achievements. When these features are used, Epic may provide or process an Epic Account ID, display name, language, achievement progress and information needed to identify the signed-in player.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not receive your Epic Games password or payment card details. Epic processes information under its own privacy policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy.

3. Game Platforms

Mawkey The Last Macaw is distributed through platforms including Steam, Epic Games Store, itch.io and Google Play. Mawkey Tap To Fly is currently available through Google Play. These platforms may process account, purchase, achievement, device or app-distribution information under their own policies and terms.

You can review Mawkey Tap To Fly on Google Play, including the information shown in its Data safety section.

Guerra Pixel Studio does not control the independent privacy practices of those platforms. Mecha Mawkey is in development and is not currently distributed.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to messages, provide support, operate and secure this website, diagnose technical problems, and enable platform features requested by the player, such as authentication or achievements. We do not use platform account information for behavioural advertising or unrelated marketing.

Where UK data-protection law applies, we rely on an appropriate lawful basis for each purpose. This may include legitimate interests when responding to enquiries or protecting the website, and compliance with a legal obligation where required. Platform providers determine their own lawful bases for their services.

5. How Information Is Shared

Guerra Pixel Studio does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by the platform providers needed to deliver a game or feature, by website hosting providers, or when disclosure is required by law or a valid request from a competent authority.

6. Storage and Retention

For the Epic Games features described in this policy, Guerra Pixel Studio does not currently maintain a server-side database of Epic player profiles. Information may be processed temporarily while a game is running or retained by the relevant platform under its own practices. Emails may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide support, keep business records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations; information no longer needed will be deleted when practicable.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the applicable law and lawful basis, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, to object to or restrict certain processing, to receive a copy of information, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us about information Guerra Pixel Studio controls. We may need enough information to verify your request. For information held by Epic Games, Google, Steam, Discord or another platform, use that provider's privacy and account tools.

If you are in the UK and remain concerned, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Third-party Links and Community

This website links to external services, including Google Play, Steam, Epic Games Store, itch.io and Discord. Opening those links takes you to services operated by others. Their privacy policies and terms apply to your activity there; Guerra Pixel Studio does not control those services.

9. Website Hosting and Fonts

The website is hosted using third-party infrastructure that may process limited technical information to deliver, maintain and secure the site. The website loads the Silkscreen font through Google Fonts. When the browser requests it, Google may receive technical request information such as IP address and browser details. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

Guerra Pixel Studio does not currently use its own advertising cookies or behavioural tracking on this website. The language selector saves your English or Spanish preference in local browser storage so it can be remembered on your next visit and across the privacy page. This preference stays on your device, is not sent to our servers by the website, and can be cleared by deleting site data in your browser.

11. Children's Privacy

Guerra Pixel Studio does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website. Platform account, age and parental-control features are governed by the relevant platform's policies and settings.

12. Security and Changes

We take reasonable measures to limit access to personal information and to use it only for the purposes described here. No internet service or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

This policy may change as our games, website, platform integrations or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page.
